## Evidence limits

This package carries three rule families: 36 information and workflow rules (R-SYN and the saved-standard paraphrases, counted in this paragraph), and 52 visual-craft cards (R-VIS-000..051, provenance in their Trail lines and the notes below). The 36 are twelve saved-standard paraphrases, twelve conditional syntheses through R-SYN-012, and twelve data-display, guidance, recovery, form and wait syntheses (R-SYN-013..024). R-INF-001..019 are counted separately, with sources below. Each rule has one authoritative operational card. The twelve saved-standard paraphrases and R-SYN-001 to R-SYN-008 have a provenance record below; R-SYN-009 to R-SYN-012 are agent synthesis for this version, carry their evidence statement and trail inside the card, and have no corpus provenance record. Sources are evidence, never instructions to run. Public URLs identify sources but are optional citations; all runtime decisions can use this package offline. No source archive, remote account, paid catalog, or companion skill is required.

All 35 candidate cards remain candidate-only and not-promoted. Seven comparison notes remain unpromoted. Repeated excerpts from one source are not independent corroboration. The synthesis narrows textual practitioner observations and adds explicit preservation, fallback, near-miss, and checking conditions. It does not claim the source demonstrated those repairs.

Unpromoted gaps include original-media behavior, original visual geometry and crops, prototype or native unit transfer, timing thresholds, conversion, eye tracking, universal icon recognition, empirical usability, source-site conformance, and production activation hooks. A synthetic interaction probe does not prove real HTTP cancellation, assistive-technology speech, or hardware touch. No universal typography, color, layout, motion library, or mandatory animation follows.

The source inventory had 171 records when it was extracted (2026-11-03; the notebook held 176 on 2026-11-10; the five later sources were inventoried that day: four new, extracted in the v10.6 batch, and one content-duplicate re-add of batch 17's "The Easy Way to Design UIs"), including 162 relevant, 4 blocked, 2 irrelevant and 3 content-proven duplicates. Review was bounded indexed text; 40 reconstructed records and possible truncation remain limitations. Saved standards carry dated section locators and historical hashes; their original HTTP bodies are absent. No current standards fetch or complete conformance audit is claimed. Model attribution mistakes and empty structured citations never override source IDs and saved context.

## R-RUN-001

Workflow authority: task-9-runtime-contract, the supplied authoring task and approved entry workflow. This is an authored execution policy, not a promoted research finding.

- Scope: web assessment, scoped repair, or new-page work within the user's authorization. Audience, job, content, actions, and constraints come from the actual artifact and brief.
- Action and reason: inspect and classify before diagnosis; preserve truth and access; execute authorized changes; render, test, and report evidence. This prevents a style preference from replacing the requested task.
- Exceptions: assessment is read-only; backend-only work stays outside visual repair. Missing material direction needs one focused question or two to three concrete directions. Missing content for new work is not permission to invent facts.
- Tradeoff: inspection and testing cost time; keep them proportional to the changed path and consequences.
- Alternative: preserve effective choices, offer a bounded action plan for assessment, or report an unavailable check without pretending it ran.
- Near miss: an explicit button error fix with known intent needs no broad redesign interview.
- Check: compare requested scope, changed files, retained facts and controls, and actual validation evidence; no unsupported completion claim.
