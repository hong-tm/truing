# Critique and repair

This diagnostic procedure applies R-RUN-001. Use it for evidence-led assessment or an authorized repair. It has no independent empirical authority.

1. Name the page surface, audience, job, and material constraints.
2. Quote or locate the observed symptom. Separate symptom from cause.
3. Name the applicable rule ID and read its complete card, including scope, exception, tradeoff, alternative, near miss, and check.
4. Choose keep, clarify, regroup, demote, or remove.
5. Preserve facts, obligations, accessibility help, rare important tasks, state recovery, and identity.
6. Apply only the authorized scope.
7. Re-render and compare the same task, states, inputs, controls, and content.

D-01 weak representation maps to R-SYN-001 in operational UI. For landing fact-condition pairing, use R-SYN-003 and R-SYN-004 within their landing scope. D-02 competing hierarchy maps to R-SYN-002. D-03 prominent low value content maps to R-SYN-003. D-04 vague labels maps to R-SYN-004. D-05 misleading async feedback maps to R-SYN-006. These diagnosis patterns are conditional. Their causes remain uncertain until observed.

Record one row per supported finding: symptom and exact page evidence; affected task and surface; possible cause and uncertainty; conditional severity; smallest repair; retained content and controls; applicable rule/source IDs; near miss; before/after check. A screenshot can show prominence but cannot prove comprehension, gaze, conversion, or response timing. Reserve high severity for demonstrated loss, false completion, missing critical information, or a consequential obstruction.

For "make this better", inspect first. If the goal remains unresolved, ask one material question such as whether the page should prioritize rapid record lookup or a management summary. Two or three directions should describe concrete changes and retained constraints. For "fix this button's error feedback only", perform that bounded fix without an unrelated design interview. For "review this", return findings and stop before editing.

Do not turn a candidate source card into a universal rule. Do not claim an empirical outcome from a visual inspection.
