# Showcase and reading

A landing page supports understanding an offer and deciding whether to act. Read the supplied facts before selecting imagery or rewriting a promise. Use [R-SYN-003 and R-SYN-004](purpose-and-content.md) for relevance and copy, and [R-SYN-002](information-and-attention.md) for emphasis. These rules allow expression when support, access, and truth survive. The operational data rule R-SYN-001 does not automatically extend to a landing page.

For editorial reading, inspect title, headings, paragraphs, source context, navigation, and any required safety content. Distinguish sequential reading from finding one section.

## R-SYN-008

Use intended reading order as a question.

- Evidence: agent-synthesis; promoted-synthesis.
- Scope: showcase-reading; following a narrative or locating supporting details. Audience: people performing the named task; domain familiarity must be inspected. Platform: web; no native unit transfer.
- Action: Sketch an intended entry, explanation and destination, then inspect whether required details are available; use relative roles without mandating three levels or a single gaze route.
- Reason: Poster advice supports a diagnostic question and expressly allows deviation. Reading-page transfer is agent synthesis.
- Exceptions: Operational monitoring has multiple valid entry points; a clear existing editorial order should remain.
- Tradeoff: A narrative route can improve intentional structure while making non-linear retrieval harder.
- Alternative: Use headings, navigation and multiple entry points instead of forcing an eye-flow formula.
- Near miss: Keep an expert dashboard or clear four-section article from an imposed three-stop path.
- Check: Check heading navigation, section findability and complete supporting text at narrow and enlarged text. Eye tracking and speed need empirical study.
- Strength and units: conditional heuristic; not empirical proof or WCAG requirement. no prescribed geometry or timing.
