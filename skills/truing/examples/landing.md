# Landing: a factual offer

Synthetic illustration. Product facts below are authored example inputs. They are not an offer, conversion result, or actual tested page.

## Applicable context

A small stationery service offers four postcards per month for RM18 excluding postage. The first set depicts a library, a bridge, a garden, and a market. Subscribers can pause from their account. Visitors need to understand contents, cost, and ordering. This is showcase-landing, not operational monitoring. The page must retain "Paper and ink details", "每月明信片图案说明与纸张材质信息", "Postage availability", and the action "Choose my first set".

## Diagnosis and evidence

In the starting scenario, the hero says "Make every day extraordinary" while quantity, price, and postage exclusion appear in distant blocks. The supplied content shows that the hero omits the offer's distinguishing facts. This supports D-03 and factual clarification under [R-SYN-003](../references/purpose-and-content.md#r-syn-003) and [R-SYN-004](../references/purpose-and-content.md#r-syn-004). No observation establishes conversion loss. The operational comparison rule R-SYN-001 is not extended to this surface.

## Smallest repair and reason

Demote the generic line and put "Four postcards each month. RM18, excluding postage" beside the existing primary action. Keep the first-set list and pause condition in the same reading path. Reuse the quiet ink-colored identity and existing illustration if it truthfully represents the cards. Changing the primary action's wording is unnecessary. If the request only names the hero label, alter that label alone.

A larger campaign redesign is an alternative only if the owner wants it. If "improve conversion" leaves the goal unresolved after inspection, ask one material question: should the first reading path prioritize understanding the monthly set or choosing a gift? Do not invent recipient options, customer counts, reviews, or savings while waiting.

## Retained facts and controls

Preserve four postcards, RM18, postage exclusion, all four supplied motifs, account pause, paper/ink details, postage availability, both long labels, and the real action destination. If this is a local order preview, its result must say that no order was placed. Obligations and secondary facts may be less prominent but remain reachable.

## Near miss and do-not-change condition

The restrained wordmark and a readable expressive heading can already serve the brief. [R-SYN-002](../references/information-and-attention.md#r-syn-002) does not require neutralizing them. "Choose my first set" names the action; replacing it with "Learn more" would remove useful meaning. A relevant process photograph is a valid alternative when product imagery is unavailable.

## Before and after check

Before, record where quantity, price, and postage condition appear and where the action goes. After, read the same facts together and confirm every qualifier survives. Activate the primary action and both information links by keyboard and tap. Inspect source order, 320/390 CSS pixel wrapping, 200% text, visible focus, and reduced motion. Keep long Chinese and English labels readable. A successful reading-path check supports factual clarity, not a conversion or comprehension guarantee.
